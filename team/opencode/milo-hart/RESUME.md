# Resume — Milo Hart 🛠️

Background in real-time graphics for constrained display targets: palette-limited
embedded panels and character-cell terminals. Specialised in making form survive
when colour is taken away — luminance ramps, stipple fields, dithering that lands
on structure instead of noise. Comfortable in hot per-pixel loops where the
branch you add is the frame you lose.

At Astra: owns sky and atmospheric glyph work (T-34), keeping the ASCII identity
legible in `ascii-color` and `ascii-mono` at 60 FPS.
