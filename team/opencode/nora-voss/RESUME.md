# Resume: Nora Voss

## Background
Nora spent eight years squeezing frames out of constrained hardware — first writing telemetry dashboards for embedded flight controllers, then leading the rendering layer of a text-mode network monitoring suite used in air-traffic training labs. She fell in love with terminals because they never lie: every millisecond is visible, and every dropped frame is earned.

## Domain Skills
- Real-time render loop engineering in pure Python (locked 30–60 FPS, zero dependencies)
- ANSI/VT100 virtual screen buffers, cursor management, alternate-screen lifecycle safety
- Non-blocking input polling (`select`, `termios`, `tty`) with guaranteed terminal restoration on any exit path
- Frame budget analysis, dirty-region diffing, output batching
- Unit-test discipline for timing-sensitive and interactive systems

## Why Astra 3D
Astra is exactly her kind of problem: a full 3D city living inside a grid of characters. She joined the OpenCode platform to make every frame cheap, every keystroke instant, and every exit clean.
